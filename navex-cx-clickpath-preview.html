What's what on this screen

  • NATIVE
    Account → Contract and Asset → Product2 are standard objects and relationships. The grids are the standard dynamic related list component.
  • NEW
    5 custom fields: Contract.Contract_Health__c, Contract.Renewal_Risk__c, Asset.Product_Health__c, Asset.Renewal_Risk__c, and critically Asset.Contract__c.
  • NEW
    The two grids on this tab — added to the record page so the custom fields are actually visible. The stock Contracts/Assets related lists (under Service) can't show them.
  • NEW
    3 Contracts and 5 Assets for Meridian.
The one real gap in Salesforce: there is no native Asset→Contract link. Asset.Contract__c is the single custom field doing genuine work here — it's what makes the company→contract→product hierarchy possible, and it's Josh's #1 hurdle.
Don't hardcode contract numbers. They were 00000103–105 and are now 00000106–108; auto-number increments every time the demo data reloads.

Where you looked: tab Success → sub-tab Account Plans  →  empty

Why there's no Account Plan — and it's a simple reason

  • NATIVE
    AccountPlan is a standard Salesforce object and it works fine in this org.
  • ALREADY HERE
    The Account Plans sub-tab is already on the page, wired to that object.
  • MISSING
    There are only 2 AccountPlan records in the entire org — "Verde Account Plan FY26" and "2026 CTW Partners Joint Business Plan", the two SDO showcase accounts. Meridian Health simply doesn't have one, because our build never created it.
Nothing is broken. The tab is empty purely because no record exists. Creating one Account Plan for Meridian would fill it — and it maps well to the "account plan with actions split across CSM and AE" story. I did not create it, since that writes to the org and you'd asked me to explain rather than change things. Say the word and it's a one-line fix.

Two things on the page you should not lean on

  • DON'T USE
    The big "Health Score 49" card (top of Details, with the sparkline). This is a CRM Analytics dashboard, not an Account field. Tech_Cloud_Health_Score__c is null and our real score is 64. The 49 is unrelated sample data and can't be changed by editing records.
  • DON'T USE
    The "Product Portfolio" treemap ("No results found"). It reads a static CSV dataset from 2024 with no recipe behind it, containing only Verde's and Accusage's rows. It genuinely cannot be populated for Meridian without overwriting another account's data. Use the Renew tab grids instead — that's exactly why we built them.
Practical advice for the demo: stay out of the Intelligence tab and don't dwell on the top Health card. Drive the health story from the highlights panel (64 / Detractor), the Scorecards sub-tab, and the Renew grids — all of which reflect real Meridian data.
